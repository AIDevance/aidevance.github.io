AIDevance File Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. This policy covers the Android app AIDevance File Manager (package com.aidevance.filemanager) published by AIDevance.

In short

  • The app has no servers of its own. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your files move only between your phone and your own Google Drive, directly, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Files on the phone

The app asks for the Android permission “All files access” so that it can show and manage the files on the phone and memory card, like any file manager. Files are read and changed only when you act on them (open, copy, move, rename, delete, share). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you copy or move a file to Google Drive or share it with another app you choose.

Google Drive and Google user data

  • What is accessed. When you press “Connect Google Drive” and allow access, the app receives access to the Google Drive of that account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): the names, sizes, dates and folders of your Drive files, their content when you open, copy, move or share them, and the account's used and total storage space.
  • Why. Only to show your Drive in the app's folder tree and to perform the actions you choose: list folders, open, copy and move files between Drive and the phone, rename, create folders, move to Drive's trash and restore, delete for good, search by name, show recent files, and save back a file you changed in another app.
  • Where it goes. Data travels directly between your phone and Google's servers. Copies of files you open or share are kept temporarily in the app's private cache on your phone. Nothing is sent to AIDevance or to anyone else.
  • Sign-in. The app never sees your Google password. Access is granted by Google through the Google account already on your phone; the access token is kept by Android on the phone.
  • Disconnect. Long-press the account in the tree and choose “Disconnect Google Drive”: the app removes the account and asks Google to revoke the access. You can also revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files in Drive are not touched.
  • No transfer, no humans, no AI training. Google user data is not transferred to third parties, is not used for advertising, is not read by people, and is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning models.

AIDevance File Manager's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Files from other clouds

“Take files from another cloud” opens the Android file window; the app receives only the files you pick there.

Updates

To check for a new version, the app downloads a small file (latest.json) and, if you agree, the new app file from GitHub (github.com/AIDevance). No personal data is sent with these requests.

Data retention and deletion

The app keeps only its settings (for example, which Google accounts are connected) and temporary cache on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them. Disconnecting an account removes it from the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
