Pulsar Direct — private P2P messenger with no sign-up

A direct messenger for Windows and Android: messages, calls, files, voice messages and groups. Your information is available only to you and the person you talk to — every message and call is end-to-end encrypted, and nothing is stored on servers. No account, no phone number, no e-mail.

All release files

Features

  • Messages without registration. If the other person is offline, the message arrives when they are back.
  • End-to-end encrypted calls. Directly between devices; through the network's servers only when there is no direct path.
  • Files and pictures. Any size, resumed after a break.
  • Voice messages and groups.
  • Several devices on one account. A computer and a phone together; your chats are on all of your devices.
  • Net. A shared network of your computers and your contacts' computers: open a folder on another computer.
  • Tunnel and screen. Go online through a contact's computer and see their screen — only with their permission.
  • PIN protection. A PIN to open the program and a separate PIN that erases all chats.
  • Updates itself. Every release is signed by the developer's key.

Getting started

  • Windows: run Pulsar-Direct-windows.exe.
  • Android: open Pulsar-Direct-arm64-v8a.apk on the phone and allow installing from this source if the phone asks.
  • Send the other person an invitation from the program — once they accept it, you share a chat.
